# Past GMU hackathon winners (what judges here have rewarded)

Scraped from the Devpost project galleries on Nov 16, 2026. "Winner" means Devpost shows a winner ribbon.

## Patterns

1. **Themed tracks reward on-theme storytelling.** PatriotHacks 2024 had a "Best Cyberpunk Theme Hack" prize. *Big Brother* won it ("tracks and graphs your focus and emotions, and is always watching"). Our dystopia track is a close relative of that prize.
2. **Safety, protection, and vulnerable users win often.** Examples: SousSafe (a hidden domestic-violence support tool in a kitchen device), CupidSecure (romance-scam detection), Sketchy (website threat scanning), GeoGuard (earthquake response), Rescue Safe (crowdsourced evacuation), and Yap (finding people in rubble).
3. **Hardware wins a lot at GMU.** Examples: WALL-E, OxiFlow, Carbin, Pulse Tester, BB Belt, HoneyCar, the research drone, and Learning Buddy. Hardware stands out in the gallery, but this event is virtual, so the hardware must show up well on camera.
4. **"AI assistant for X" is crowded.** At least 8 GMU chat assistants have appeared since 2023 (course planners, campus helpers, study bots). They rarely place unless the angle is sharp. Avoid a plain chatbot.
5. **Multi-agent and agentic systems are a 2026 trend.** Examples: Peak (a multi-agent athlete advisor), s24 (an autonomous trading agent), and PulseLink (a multi-agent workforce). An agentic demo shows technical depth.
