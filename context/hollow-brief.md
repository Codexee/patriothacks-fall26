# Track brief: "Life underground" (the Hollow)

Source: the organizers' Google Doc (https://docs.google.com/document/d/1sz_UdwRzvyL1dVbcbqLnUdJwVHvOUWARg4B79VmKgaE), read on Tue Nov 17, 2026. **This brief replaces the general track text in `event.md`.** All earlier ideas (Off the Record, Pinpoint, Flock) do not fit it.

## The world (summary)

- Year 2087. The surface is unlivable (superstorms, fires, poisoned air). Machines that humans left behind still run the dead cities.
- 2 billion people live in **the Hollow**: cities in old mines and subway tunnels, stacked in levels. Nobody has been to the surface in 30 years.
- Everything is rationed. Power comes from aging geothermal plants. Water is recycled. Food grows under lamps in communal farms. Light is a paid utility.
- **The Directorate** (the government) controls air, the network, and all movement between levels.
- **Upper levels** get light, fresh air, and space. **Deep levels** are darker, hotter, and sicker, and blackouts hit them first.
- The systems are failing: blackouts last for days, lower-level water recyclers break, lung sickness spreads, and **the Directorate's public numbers do not match what people see**.
- Many residents believe the surface has healed and the Directorate hides it. The way up is overrun by machines.

## The 15 problems

| # | Area | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Power | Warn people before the power or air cuts out. |
| 2 | Power | Share power, water, and food fairly when there is not enough. |
| 3 | Environment | Keep the lower levels livable as heat rises and cooling fails. |
| 4 | Environment | Catch bad water before people drink it. |
| 5 | Environment | Keep tunnels safe from sewage and floods that cut off neighborhoods. |
| 6 | Crowding | Find or make more space, or help people share it. |
| 7 | Communication | Organize, share information, and call for help when the Directorate shuts the network. |
| 8 | Communication | Reconnect families split across levels during a blackout. |
| 9 | Health | Track the real lung-sickness outbreak and get supplies where needed. |
| 10 | Health | Get basic care to people who cannot reach a clinic. |
| 11 | Health | Track and treat what a life without sunlight does to sleep, mood, and the body. |
| 12 | Food | Help a cut-off neighborhood grow and share its own food. |
| 13 | Surface | Prove whether the surface is safe, and get the true answer to everyone. |
| 14 | Surface | Decide which option (suits, domes, sealed outposts) works, and who goes first. |
| 15 | Surface | Plan the move of hundreds of millions back to the surface. |

## What the organizers say about scope

- "Ideally, one well-connected solution addresses several of these problems at the same time, and that can strengthen your project in judging."
- "Pick one question or combine a few." Both paths are valid: make life underground sustainable, or help people return to the surface.
