## The world (summary)

- **Year 2087.** Storms, fires and poisoned air made the surface unlivable. About 2 billion people live in **the Hollow**: cities in old mines and subway tunnels, stacked in levels under the dead cities.
- **The surface** is run by machines that humans left behind. They answer to no one. Nobody has gone up in 30 years.
- **Rationing everywhere.** Aging geothermal plants supply the power. Water is recycled again and again. Food grows under lamps on communal farms. Light is a paid utility.
- **The Directorate** is the government. It controls the air supply, the network, and all movement between levels.
- **Inequality by depth.** Upper levels get light, air and space. Deep levels are darker, hotter and sicker, and blackouts hit them first.
- **Now the systems are failing.** Blackouts last for days, the lower-level water recyclers are breaking down, and lung sickness is spreading. **The Directorate's public numbers don't match what people see.**
- **The surface question.** Many residents believe the surface has healed and that the Directorate hides it. The machines block the way up.
- **The two paths:** make life underground more sustainable, or help people get back to the surface. The brief says both paths are valid.

## What the brief rewards (our reading)

1. **Coverage across problems.** The brief says one well-connected solution that addresses several problems "can strengthen your project in judging." Count which problem numbers a project covers, and say them in the pitch.
2. **A clear choice of path.** Either sustain life underground, or return to the surface.
3. **On-world storytelling.** Projects should speak the world's language: levels, the Directorate, blackouts, the deep levels.

## Problem clusters (for ideation)

- **Truth vs the Directorate:** 9, 13 (and 4, through water tests). Independent measurement against official numbers.
- **Networks without the Directorate:** 7, 8 (and 1, through alerts spreading peer to peer).
- **Infrastructure sensing and forecasting:** 1, 3, 4, 5. Sensors plus prediction.
- **Fair allocation:** 2, 6, 12. Algorithms and ledgers.
- **Care:** 10, 11.
- **Return to the surface:** 13, 14, 15. Simulation and planning.
